# How to vote

[Verified members](./) with Pathfinder, Navigator and Pilot role are eligible to vote for projects in the [Build Award Open Track](../../scf-awards/build-award/open-track.md) during the Community Vote phase. Community Vote in SCF leverages [Neural Quorum Goverance](../neural-quorum-governance/) to allocate voting power based on reputation and also allows for flexible delegation. If you're a verified SCF member, your vote (or your delegation) directly shapes where awards go.&#x20;

### 1. Log into the SCF Dashboard

Visit [communityfund.stellar.org](https://communityfund.stellar.org), click on the Dashboard button in the top right corner, and log in with Discord. You'll need to have joined the [Stellar Developers Discord](https://discord.gg/stellardev) in order to log in.&#x20;

We highly recommend using your computer (not phone or tablet) as well as a browser with extensions (such as Google Chrome) to interact with the SCF Dashboard.

### 2. Make sure you're verified with Pathfinder and up

Your membership tier determines what you can do during Community Vote. You can find out where you are in the SCF Dashboard, under 'Verified Tier'. There are four tiers, assigned as Discord roles:

<table><thead><tr><th width="192.9453125">Tier</th><th>What you can do during Community Vote</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><strong>Verified</strong></td><td>View discussions in <code>#verified-panel</code> (no voting)</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Pathfinder</strong></td><td>Delegate your votes to trusted delegates</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Navigator</strong></td><td>Vote directly on projects</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Pilot</strong></td><td>Vote directly + serve as a <a href="../../scf-awards/build-award/quarterly-governance-process.md">quarterly delegate</a></td></tr></tbody></table>

### 3. Set up your (anonymous) Voting Wallet

Head to the 'Community Vote' tab in the SCF Dashboard and add a browser wallet account (like [Freighter](https://freighter.app/)) that's anonymous (different from the public wallet you linked as part of registration). This is important since the wallet address will be publicly traceable in the NQG published data, and we want to avoid linking back to any known identity (like your Discord account). If you need someone to fund the minimum account balance, you can reach out to Emir (emir0000) in the [Stellar Developers Discord](https://discord.gg/stellardev).

### 4. Review your voting weight and assign trust to others

Your voting weight is decided by [Neural Quorum Governance](../neural-quorum-governance/), on things like voting history, your Discord roles and trust given by others. If you haven't voted before, it's likely you may not yet have a voting weight (also referred to as NQG score) assigned yet. You can find more information about your voting weight in the 'Voting History' tab in the SCF Dashboard. \
\
In addition, we ask each voter prior to voting in a round to update at their trusted user list in the 'Delegates and Trust' tab in the SCF Dashboard. Adding trust to other community members will increase their relative voting weight.

### 5. Select your delegates quorum

Not everyone may have the right expertise or availability to vote on every project in a round, and that's why we have the option to delegate your votes to a quorum of [quarterly selected delegates](../../scf-awards/build-award/quarterly-governance-process.md) (see more information on how this works in [Neural Quorum Governance](../neural-quorum-governance/)).

In the 'Community Vote' tab of the SCF Dashboard after connecting your wallet, you can select 7 to 15 delegates of your choice. To learn more about each of them, visit the quarterly governance thread of that quarter in #scf-governance the [Stellar Developers Discord](https://discord.gg/stellardev). \
\
SCF's implementation of Quorum Delegation is on purpose designed to be sensitive to downvotes. To have your delegate vote approve of an award decision, or 'yes', 66.7% of your selected delegates (e.g. 5 out of 7) needs to approve. Otherwise, your delegate vote will become 'no'.

### 6. Review the submissions and vote

All eligible Open Track submissions are listed on the 'Community Vote' tab in the SCF Dashboard. Submissions have already passed prescreen and panel review by the time they reach Community Vote, and teams may have revised based on reviewer feedback.

What to look for depends on what you believe is important, but generally voters look for ecosystem value, team capability, and technical feasibility.

You can either choose to delegate your votes to your selected quorum or to vote directly. By default, all decisions are set to "Delegate", which you can undo by clicking "Remove Delegate Vote". You won't be able to do this if you're a Pathfinder.<br>

* **If you're an Open Track Delegate**: you're expected to vote either ‘👍’ to approve or ‘👎’ to disapprove for all projects.
* **If you're a Pilot or a Navigator:** Choose to either add a ‘👍’ to approve, a ‘👎’ to disapprove, or ‘Delegate’ to defer the opinion to someone else. Please note that approving means the output from this submission brings substantial value to the Stellar ecosystem, doesn’t have any red flags, the team is ready to build, the budget makes sense, and the outlined deliverables are the right next steps for this project.
* **If you’re a Pathfinder**: You'll be only able to delegate your votes. Simply select your Delegates (min. 7) in order of importance and submit your ballot.

Once you’re finished adding your votes, click to open the ‘Your Ballot’ located at the lower right corner of your screen. Submit your ballot once you’re ready before the voting deadline!

### 7. Delegate Discussion

After community vote, the quarterly delegates discuss the final results and make a decision on which projects to fund. Community Vote isn't yet determinative on award allocation, but the delegates try to take in community signal as much as possible while considering what actual feedback was shared.

### 8. Follow the stats and reports

Each round, a round recap is published on the [Stellar Community Medium](https://medium.com/stellar-community) announcing the final award recipients for each track as well as a deeper dive into the voting results. You can also check out the 'Analytics' tab in the SCF Dashboard for statistics per round as well as across multiple rounds.

Any questions, or running into issues? Ask them in scf-general in the [Stellar Developers Discord](https://discord.gg/stellardev).&#x20;
